Why retail buyers work with STWADD

We are a factory and an agent. For drinkware we supply you directly from our own lines at factory-bottom price. For everything else your shelves need, our buying office sources from vetted external factories, consolidates, inspects and ships. That combination — factory + agent — is what most pure sourcing agents cannot offer.

Transparent cost

A flat 3–5% commission billed against the factory invoice — far below the 5–15% (or hidden 15–30%) trading-company markup typical elsewhere. No separate hidden margin. Sample coordination, pre-shipment AQL inspection and consolidation warehousing are included.

One container, many factories

Mixed-category orders from 5–10 vetted factories per category, consolidated into a single shipment. One PO, one bill of lading, one customs set.

Quality you can verify

In-house AQL 2.5 pre-shipment inspection with a downloadable report and packaging photos before the container loads. Annual factory audits on fire safety, social compliance and capacity. Third-party SGS / BV on request.

Your brand stays on top

Private-label and OEM packaging: custom logo, retail-ready color boxes, UPC/EAN barcodes, gift-set assembly. Our name stays off your product.

How the buying office works

1. Brief & RFQ

You send specs / target shelf price. We issue RFQs to vetted factories and compare quotes side by side.

2. Match & sample

We shortlist 5–10 factories per category, coordinate samples, and confirm pre-production approval.

3. Consolidate

Mixed-category goods aggregated in our warehouse into one container (FCL consolidation).

4. Inspect

AQL 2.5 pre-shipment inspection with report and photos before loading.

5. Private label

Custom logo, retail-ready packaging, UPC/EAN, gift-set assembly.

6. Ship

We arrange ocean freight and the full document set (CI, PL, B/L, certificates) to your port. FOB / EXW / assist with clearance.

Who this is for: supermarket, convenience, dollar and discount retail chains and buying groups with a minimum consolidated project value of about US$20,000 per container. For drinkware alone, our factory MOQ starts from ~500 pieces and is negotiable.

Why retail buyers work with STWADD

We have both sides of the relationship. For drinkware we work directly with audited partner factories in Yongkang and take responsibility for the production we specify, so quotes are factory-direct. For everything else your shelves need, the buying office sources from vetted factories, consolidates, inspects and ships. Being the buyer on both sides is what pure sourcing agents cannot offer.

Transparent cost

A flat 3–5% commission billed against the factory invoice — far below the 5–15% (or hidden 15–30%) trading-company markup typical elsewhere. No separate hidden margin. Sample coordination, pre-shipment AQL inspection and consolidation warehousing are included.

One container, many factories

Mixed-category orders from 5–10 vetted factories per category, consolidated into a single shipment. One PO, one bill of lading, one customs set.

Quality you can verify

In-house AQL 2.5 pre-shipment inspection with a downloadable report and packaging photos before the container loads. Annual factory audits on fire safety, social compliance and capacity. Third-party SGS / BV on request.

Your brand stays on top

Private-label and OEM packaging: custom logo, retail-ready color boxes, UPC/EAN barcodes, gift-set assembly. Our name stays off your product.

The AI + human loop

Pure-AI sourcing over-promises — it can't verify a factory or touch a sample. Pure-human sourcing is slow and expensive. STWADD runs a hybrid: AI does the repetitive matching and indicative pricing across thousands of 1688 SKUs; a human does the part that actually de-risks the order.

1. You upload a list

A spreadsheet of SKUs, target shelf price and quantity. Or just describe what your shelves need.

2. AI matches & prices

Our engine reads the list, finds candidate SKUs on 1688, and returns indicative lowest prices and product images per line.

3. Human verifies

A buyer confirms each line on 1688 (live stock, real supplier), checks the factory, and negotiates the final cost.

4. We consolidate & inspect

Mixed categories aggregated into one container, AQL 2.5 inspection, private-label packaging, shipped to your port.

Why this beats either extreme: AI compresses the search from days to minutes and surfaces prices a human buyer would never click through; the human catches the listings that are out of stock, mis-spec'd, or from an unverified supplier. The result is a priced, verified shortlist you can act on.

How the buying office works

1. Brief & RFQ

You send specs / target shelf price. We issue RFQs to vetted factories and compare quotes side by side.

2. Match & sample

We shortlist 5–10 factories per category, coordinate samples, and confirm pre-production approval.

3. Consolidate

Mixed-category goods aggregated in our warehouse into one container (FCL consolidation).

4. Inspect

AQL 2.5 pre-shipment inspection with report and photos before loading.

5. Private label

Custom logo, retail-ready packaging, UPC/EAN, gift-set assembly.

6. Ship

We arrange ocean freight and the full document set (CI, PL, B/L, certificates) to your port. FOB / EXW / assist with clearance.

Who this is for: supermarket, convenience, dollar and discount retail chains and buying groups with a minimum consolidated project value of about US$20,000 per container. For drinkware alone, drinkware MOQ starts from ~500 pieces and is negotiable.
